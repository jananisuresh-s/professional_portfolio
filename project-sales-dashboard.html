Sales Performance Dashboard

Data Analysis  /  Power BI

An end-to-end sales analytics project — cleaning nearly 3,100 raw sales records, analyzing trends with SQL, and building an interactive Power BI dashboard covering revenue, profit, top products, regional performance, and monthly trends.

View on GitHub

Why I built this

I wanted a project that went through the full data analyst workflow end to end — starting from messy, real-world-style raw data, not a pre-cleaned dataset. Raw sales exports are rarely usable as-is: missing values, inconsistent casing, stray currency symbols, and incomplete date fields all show up here, so the cleaning stage had to earn the analysis that came after it.

What it does

The project moves through three stages: cleaning, SQL analysis, and dashboarding.

  • Data cleaning — standardized column headers, trimmed and title-cased text fields, parsed order dates into year/quarter/month/day features, stripped currency formatting from numeric fields, and dropped rows missing quantity or revenue
  • Recalculated metrics — rebuilt total cost, revenue, profit, and profit margin from quantity and unit price/cost directly, rather than trusting the raw figures
  • SQL analysis — loaded the cleaned data into SQLite and queried executive KPIs, salesperson leaderboard, regional performance, category profitability, and top 5 profitable products
  • Power BI dashboard — an interactive dashboard surfacing revenue, profit, top products, regional performance, and monthly trends

Key findings

Across 2,997 cleaned records, the dataset totals roughly ₹78.1L in revenue and ₹33.0L in profit. Revenue is fairly evenly spread across regions, with South leading and North lowest. Home & Kitchen edges out Furniture and Electronics as the most profitable category, with Stationery and Apparel close behind — no single category dominates, which made the regional and category breakdowns more useful than a single top-line number.

My role
